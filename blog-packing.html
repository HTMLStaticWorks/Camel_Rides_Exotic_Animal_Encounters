Desert Essentials: What to Pack

A comprehensive guide on gear and clothing to ensure your majestic trail adventure is comfortable and safe.

The desert is a land of extremes. While the days can be scorching, the evening temperatures often drop rapidly. Being prepared with the right gear is the difference between an okay trip and a majestic one.

Sun Protection is Key

A wide-brimmed hat, polarized sunglasses, and breathable long sleeves are your best friends. We also recommend reef-safe sunscreen to protect both your skin and our delicate desert ecosystem.

"Dress for the environment, not just the photo. Comfort is the ultimate luxury on the trail."

Footwear and Hydration

Closed-toe shoes are mandatory for camel rides. While we provide plenty of water on all tours, bringing your own reusable insulated bottle is highly encouraged and helps reduce plastic waste.
