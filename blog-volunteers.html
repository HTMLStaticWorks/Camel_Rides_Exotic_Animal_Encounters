Volunteer Stories: Life at the Farm

Meet the passionate people who help care for our animals every day and learn why they chose this path.

Majestic Trails is powered by a community of dedicated volunteers. From biology students to retired adventurers, these individuals bring a wealth of knowledge and passion to our farm.

A Day in the Life

A volunteer's day starts before dawn. Tasks range from preparing specialized feed mixes to leading educational sessions for school groups. It's hard work, but the bond formed with the animals makes it all worthwhile.

"Working with the camels has taught me more about patience and non-verbal communication than any textbook ever could."

Join the Herd

We are always looking for dedicated individuals to join our team. Whether you have experience with animals or just a willingness to learn, there's a place for you on the majestic trail.
