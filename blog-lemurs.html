The Incredible World of Lemurs

Learn about our curious lemur colony and their unique social structures in the heart of our oasis.

Lemurs are some of the most charismatic and misunderstood primates on Earth. At Majestic Trails, we provide a sanctuary for several species, allowing them to thrive in a protected, naturalistic environment.

Sun-Worshipping Socialites

If you visit early in the morning, you'll see our ring-tailed lemurs sitting in their famous "yoga" pose, soaking up the sun's rays to warm their bodies after a cool desert night.

"Every lemur has a distinct personality—from the mischievous troop leader to the gentle nursery caregivers."

Conservation efforts

Our lemur program is focused on education. By allowing visitors to observe these animals up close, we hope to inspire action for their endangered cousins in the wild of Madagascar.
