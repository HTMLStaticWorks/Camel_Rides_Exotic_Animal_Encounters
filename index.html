Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our treks, farm visits, and animal care policies.

Are camel rides safe for young children?

Yes! We have specialized saddles for children and our guides walk alongside younger riders at all times. Safety is our top priority.

What should I wear for my trek?

We recommend comfortable, loose-fitting clothing and closed-toe shoes. A hat and sunscreen are essential for the desert sun.

How do you ensure the camels are well treated?

We follow a strict ethical care policy. Our camels work limited hours, receive daily health checks, and have full rest days in between treks.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes, we highly recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance as our popular sunset and sunrise treks fill up quickly.
