Ethical Camel Care: Behind the Scenes

At Majestic Trails, our commitment to animal welfare is the foundation of everything we do. Here's a look at the daily lives of our camels.

Ethical care starts with understanding the biological and psychological needs of the animal. Our camels aren't just "working animals"; they are members of our extended family. Each day begins with a thorough health check and a personalized nutrition plan.

Natural Living Conditions

We've designed our enclosures to mimic the natural environments of the high desert, providing ample space for movement, social interaction, and rest. We follow a strict "rotation" policy, ensuring no camel works more than four hours a day, with full rest days in between.

"Tourism should never come at the cost of animal dignity. Our mission is to bridge the gap between human curiosity and animal respect."

Enrichment and Bonding

Beyond physical care, mental stimulation is vital. We engage our camels in bonding exercises with our professional handlers, fostering trust and reducing stress during interactions with visitors.
