Visit the Farm

Not just rides—come and meet our entire exotic family in a beautiful outdoor setting.

What to Expect

Our farm is designed for relaxation and education. We host a variety of exotic species from across the globe, all cared for by our professional zoological team.

Safety Guidelines

Your safety and the well-being of our animals are our top priorities.

Gentle Interactions

Always approach animals from the side and never attempt to touch their faces without a guide's permission.

Quiet Atmosphere

Loud noises can startle exotic species. We ask all visitors to maintain a calm and quiet demeanor.

Stay on Designated Paths

Our farm is a delicate ecosystem. Please remain on marked trails to protect native flora and fauna.
