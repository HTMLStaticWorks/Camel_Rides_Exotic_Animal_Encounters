Trail Essentials

Quick answers to prepare you for the trek.

What should I wear?

Lightweight, breathable clothing and closed-toe shoes are essential. We provide sun hats if needed.

Is there an age limit?

We welcome explorers of all ages. Children under 5 ride with a guardian on specialized saddles.

How safe are the treks?

Safety is our priority. Every camel is trained for years, and our guides are certified in desert first aid.
